# Northlight Vault — Cloud File Browser

All your cloud storage. One place. No switching tabs.

---

## What it is

Northlight Vault is a Windows desktop app that lets you browse, search, and open files across multiple cloud storage accounts — without logging in and out or juggling browser tabs.

Connect your Google Drive, Microsoft OneDrive, and Dropbox accounts and see everything in one place. Search across all of them at once. Click any file to open it.

---

## Supported providers

- Google Drive
- Microsoft OneDrive
- Dropbox

---

## Current limitations

- **Windows only** — Mac and Linux are not supported in v1.0.0
- **Read-only** — you can browse and open files, but not upload, edit, move, or delete
- **No offline access** — the app requires an internet connection to load file lists

---

## Installation

1. Download **Northlight Vault Setup 1.0.0.exe** from the [Releases](../../releases) page
2. Run the installer and follow the prompts
3. Launch Northlight Vault from your desktop or Start menu

---

## Connecting accounts

1. Open Northlight Vault
2. Click **+ Connect Google Drive**, **+ Connect OneDrive**, or **+ Connect Dropbox** in the sidebar
3. Sign in through the browser window that opens
4. Your account will appear in the sidebar once connected

You can connect multiple accounts from the same or different providers. To disconnect an account, click the **×** next to it in the sidebar.

---

## Privacy

Northlight Vault does not collect or transmit your files or personal information. Authentication tokens are stored locally in the Windows Credential Store and never leave your device. Crash reports are sent to Sentry (EU servers) when the app encounters an error.

See the full [Privacy Policy](PRIVACY_POLICY.md) for details.
